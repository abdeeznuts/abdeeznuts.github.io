Cancellations and refunds.

Book now flights and hotels. The rules shown before you pay apply. Non-refundable fares and rooms can’t be refunded. Refundable ones are refunded to the card you paid with, less any airline or hotel penalty. Card refunds usually take 5–10 business days to reach your statement.

Changes to a flight. Date and name changes depend on the fare rules and cost the airline’s change fee and any fare difference, plus our booking partner’s change fee. Your advisor tells you the full cost before anything is changed.

If the airline cancels. We contact you about a refund or a new flight.

Packages and advisor bookings. Your advisor confirms the cancellation terms in writing before you pay. Visa fees, and charges a hotel, airline or transport company won’t return, can’t be refunded once paid to them. Refunds for bank transfers go back to the account you paid from.

How to cancel or change. Message us with your booking reference (it starts with UW-). You can find it in My trips.
