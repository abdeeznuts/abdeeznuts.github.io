Terms of use and booking.

These terms apply when you use this app to plan and book travel with Uniworld Travel & Tours (Private) Limited, Karachi, Pakistan (IATA 273-4-4376).

Requests. A request is not a booking. A booking exists only after a UniWorld advisor confirms it with you, or after a Book now payment succeeds.

Book now. Fares and hotels marked Book now are booked through our partner Nuitée (LiteAPI), which takes the card payment on its secure form as merchant of record, so its name may appear on your card statement. The fare or room rules shown before you pay, such as refundable or not and baggage, are part of your booking.

Prices. Live prices are what Google Flights, Google Hotels or our booking partner showed at that moment and can change until you pay. If a Book now price changes before payment, the app shows the new price and you choose whether to continue. Your advisor confirms the final price, what’s included and the payment terms before you pay.

Payment. Pay only through the app’s card form, the payment link your advisor sends, or UniWorld’s bank details. UniWorld never asks for card numbers by message or phone.

Names and documents. Names must match the passport exactly. You are responsible for valid passports, visas, health requirements and any permits your trip needs, including Saudi Arabia’s rules for Umrah. Your advisor can guide you.

Changes and cancellations. See Cancellations and refunds.

Suppliers. Flights, hotels and transport are provided by airlines, hotels and transport companies under their own conditions. Schedule changes and cancellations by them are outside UniWorld’s control; we help you with a refund or a new option when they happen.

Cheaper ways to fly. Split tickets are two separate bookings, and hidden-city fares are shown for information only. UniWorld never issues hidden-city tickets.
