Your details. Your trips.

This policy explains how Uniworld Travel & Tours (Private) Limited (“UniWorld”, “we”) uses the details you give us in this app.

What we receive. When you send a request: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email if you add it, your notes and the trip details. When you book a Book now fare or hotel: the travelers’ names, dates of birth, gender, nationality and, for international flights, passport details. Your wish list and saved itineraries are kept online for this device. The app uses a random device code instead of an account.

Why. To arrange your travel, book it with airlines and hotels, and contact you about your trips.

Who sees it. The UniWorld team. For Book now bookings, the travelers’ details go to our booking partner Nuitée (LiteAPI) and on to the airline or hotel, because they are needed to issue the booking; passport numbers are not stored by UniWorld. Live prices are checked with Google Flights and Google Hotels without your personal details.

Payments. Card payments happen on the payment provider’s secure form or page. The app never sees or stores card numbers. Bank transfers go straight to UniWorld’s account.

How long. We keep your request and booking details while we need them for your trip and for our legal and accounting records. You can ask us to delete them sooner where the law allows.

On this phone. The app remembers your currency, contact details, wish list and drafts in this browser so you don’t have to type them again. Clearing the browser’s data removes them. Phone alerts are optional and can be turned off at any time.

No ads. The app has no advertising or analytics trackers. Images and fonts load from Unsplash, Google and Wikimedia, which receive standard web requests such as your IP address.

Children. Bookings are made by adults. A parent or guardian provides a child’s travel details.

Your choices. To see, correct or delete your details, contact us below.
